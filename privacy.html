Magnitude: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Magnitude is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Magnitude (@magnitudescience) to upload the channel's own videos and read the channel's own analytics.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel, and to read view, watch-time and retention statistics for the owner's own videos.

What it stores

The OAuth token and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Third parties

The tool talks only to Google and YouTube APIs. Use of those APIs is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the Magnitude YouTube channel: youtube.com/@magnitudescience
